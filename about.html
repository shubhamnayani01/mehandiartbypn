Started with a single cone and a steady hand on the lanes of old Pandeshra. PN didn't learn mehandi in any fancy academy — she learned it from watching her nani, practising on neighbours, and perfecting designs through thousands of real hands.

Today, Mehandi Art by PN is the go-to name for brides, functions, and everyday girls who want art that's real, bold, and long lasting. No templates. No shortcuts. Every stroke is freehanded.

We've inked over 20+ brides across Pandeshra and beyond. The cone is always ready. The henna is always fresh.

Early 2025

The First Cone

Started practising mehandi at home, learning traditional patterns from family elders. Experimented with different henna pastes and found the perfect organic mix.

Mid 2025

First Clients

Word spread through the neighbourhood. Friends, relatives, and neighbours started booking for small functions. Every design was different — no stencils from day one.

Late 2025

Bridal Specialist

Completed first 10 bridal mehandi sessions. Started getting bookings from across Pandeshra. Introduced glitter mehandi and feet design specializations.

2026

Growing Strong

20+ brides served, event bookings increasing, and a growing Instagram following. Added home visit service across all Pandeshra areas. Building the brand one hand at a time.
