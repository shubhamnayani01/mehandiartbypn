✨ Aftercare & Results

Follow these tips for the darkest possible stain:

  • Keep the paste on for at least 6-8 hours (overnight is best)
  • Apply our sugar-lemon sealant (we provide this)
  • Avoid water for 12-24 hours after removing the paste
  • Keep hands warm — warmth darkens the stain
  • Apply natural oil (coconut or mustard) after removing paste

The stain starts orange and deepens to dark brown/maroon over 24-48 hours.

With proper aftercare, our natural henna stain lasts 1-3 weeks. Bridal mehandi (with heavier paste application) can last up to 3 weeks. The stain gradually fades as your skin naturally exfoliates. Avoid chlorine pools, bleach, and excessive handwashing with harsh soaps to extend the life of your design.
